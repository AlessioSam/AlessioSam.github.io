Biography

I am a Senior AI Researcher at Translated in Rome, where I work on pre-training multilingual large language models (data mixtures, training curricula and cross-lingual evaluation) within the DVPS Horizon Europe project. I received my PhD in Data Science from Sapienza University of Rome, where I worked with Fabio Galasso in the PINlab on modelling humans: forecasting how people move, synthesising realistic motion, and understanding how humans interact with robots, scenes and each other. Before Translated, I was Lead Researcher at ItalAI and a research intern at Panasonic North America in Mountain View, collaborating with BAIR (UC Berkeley) and Stanford.

My work spans human motion forecasting and generation, human–robot collaboration, machine unlearning, and the evaluation of large language models on long-context code and time series. It has appeared at ICCV, ECCV, ICLR, AAAI, ACL, BMVC and WACV — see research.
