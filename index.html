What I work on

From how people move
to how they speak.

I started in computer vision, teaching models to forecast and generate human motion. Today at Translated I pre-train multilingual large language models, working on data mixtures, training curricula and cross-lingual evaluation.

Now
🌍

Multilingual LLMs

Pre-training language models from scratch for many languages, within the DVPS Horizon Europe project. Benchmarks for long-context code and time series.

🏃

Human motion

Forecasting, synthesising and understanding human movement, from pose forecasting with GCNs to length-aware text-to-motion diffusion.

🤖

Humans & machines

Robots that collaborate safely with people and navigate crowds, plus making generative models forget what they shouldn't know.
